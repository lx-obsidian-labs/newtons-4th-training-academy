# Institutional content governance

> Engineering documentation maintained by [LX Obsidian Labs](https://lxobsidian.co.za). Project: Newton’s 4th Training Academy. Institutional content remains subject to academy approval and applicable rights.

The academy owns approval of programme availability, fees, entry requirements, certification, accreditation scope and safety instruction. The developer owns technical publication and correction workflows.

## Publication process
1. Record proposed change and authoritative source.
2. Obtain academy approval and qualified safety review where applicable.
3. Update catalogue, visual metadata, learning topics and printable materials together.
4. Run integrity checks and preview affected pages.
5. Record approval, commit and publication date.

Never infer accreditation from a course name or promise employment. Illustrations must be technically reviewed; photographs require documented usage rights. Learning materials are introductory unless formally approved. Remove or correct disputed claims promptly.
