# Portfolio case study — Newton’s 4th Training Academy

## Problem
Prospective vocational learners need to identify equipment, compare training topics and prepare for admissions even when technical terminology is unfamiliar.

## Solution
A static-first React platform combines visual discovery, aliases, programme detail pages, comparison, printable handbooks and introductory practice. Local shortlists and preparation progress avoid mandatory accounts.

## Engineering contribution
Catalogue-to-visual integrity checks, accessible SVG assets, responsive presentation, explicit privacy boundaries, deployment runbook and CI build gates.

## Trade-offs
Static delivery reduces data exposure and complexity but cannot provide official academic records. Original illustrations reduce licensing and bandwidth concerns but need technical review and may be less recognisable than photographs.

## Evidence and outcomes
Source inspection confirms 42 catalogue entries with visual and topic coverage. No measured conversion, performance or learning-outcome improvement is claimed. Capture approved screenshots, CI results and usability findings before publishing quantified outcomes.

## Next iteration
Modular domain data, behavioural tests, verified photography and secure institutional workflows.
