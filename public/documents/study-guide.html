Course study and revision pack

Student Learning Centre • Introductory edition

Important: Supplementary educational material only. Not an accredited curriculum, official assessment, manufacturer operating manual or authority to operate machinery. Training must follow the provider's approved curriculum, equipment-specific OEM manual, site procedures and qualified supervision.

1. Learning objectives

By the end of the introductory study session, learners should be able to explain the purpose of the course, identify key terminology, describe common workplace hazards, explain relevant PPE and safe-work principles, and identify questions to discuss with a qualified instructor.

2. Course introduction

Core concepts to research with your instructor

  1. What the equipment, trade or discipline is used for in the workplace
  2. Important components, tools and terminology
  3. Workplace risk assessment and hazard reporting
  4. Roles, responsibilities, communication and supervision
  5. Inspection, defects and reporting requirements
  6. Environmental controls, housekeeping and emergency response

3. Safety and compliance study notes

  • Follow applicable South African legislation, workplace procedures and training requirements.
  • Use only equipment and tasks for which you have been trained and authorised.
  • Follow the actual equipment manufacturer's instructions and site-specific risk assessments.
  • Do not bypass safety devices, perform unauthorised repairs or attempt hazardous operations.
  • Report defects, unsafe conditions, incidents and near misses promptly.
  • For lifting, mining and mobile equipment, confirm exclusion zones, ground conditions, communication methods and emergency arrangements with a competent supervisor.

4. Revision questions

  1. Explain the purpose and common applications of this course.
  2. List five hazards that could arise in the relevant workplace.
  3. What information should be checked before starting a practical task?
  4. What would you do if you noticed damaged equipment or an unsafe condition?
  5. Why are competent supervision and manufacturer instructions essential?
  6. Describe the difference between routine inspection and an unauthorised repair.
  7. What information should be included in a hazard or incident report?
  8. Identify three questions you would ask your instructor before practical training.
