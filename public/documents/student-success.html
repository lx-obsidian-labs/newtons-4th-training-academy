Career readiness

Build your CV

Include contact details, a short professional profile, education, verified training, relevant experience and references with permission. Never claim a licence or certificate you have not earned.

Prepare for interviews

Use the STAR method: situation, task, action and result. Prepare an example of teamwork, problem solving and following safety instructions.

Evidence portfolio

Keep authorised assessment records, certificates, supervisor feedback and a log of learning activities. Do not photograph restricted workplaces without permission.

Job search checklist

Training does not guarantee employment. Never pay an unverified recruiter for a job offer.
