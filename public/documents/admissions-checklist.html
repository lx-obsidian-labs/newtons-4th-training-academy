NEWTON'S 4TH TRAINING ACADEMY

Admissions checklist

Middelburg · Mpumalanga · South Africa

Before registration

  • □ Choose a programme and ask about current availability.
  • □ Confirm minimum age, entry requirements and any medical fitness requirements.
  • □ Ask which identity and educational documents are required.
  • □ Request a written quotation, payment terms and refund policy.
  • □ Verify the applicable accreditation and certificate before payment.
  • □ Confirm training dates, venue, equipment and assessment arrangements.
  • □ Keep copies of written registration confirmation and payment receipts.

Do not send sensitive identity documents through an unverified channel. Contact admissions for approved submission instructions.
