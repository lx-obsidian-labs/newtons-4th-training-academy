ADMISSIONS / NEXT STEPS

How to apply for training.

A straightforward journey from choosing a programme to confirming your registration.

01

Explore

Choose a training discipline and shortlist relevant programmes.

02

Enquire

Ask for current dates, prerequisites, fees, venue, assessment and certification.

03

Confirm

Review written terms, verify payment details and complete the academy's official registration process.

Questions worth asking

  • What is the full course title and applicable qualification or skills programme?
  • Which accreditation, provider approval or assessment body applies to this specific programme?
  • What prior learning, age, literacy, medical or safety requirements apply?
  • How many days or hours are theory and supervised practical learning?
  • What is included in the fee, and what are the cancellation or refund terms?
  • Which certificate or statement of results is issued, and by whom?

PREPARATION / STUDENT SUPPORT

Prepare to learn with confidence.

Use this checklist to prepare for your first conversation with admissions and your eventual training programme.

Before registration

  • □ Confirm the exact programme and its entry requirements.
  • □ Request the current course outline, fees and training schedule in writing.
  • □ Verify the training venue and any practical training arrangements.
  • □ Ask what identification or supporting documents are required and how to submit them securely.
  • □ Review payment, refund and cancellation terms before making payment.

For learning

  • □ Notebook or exercise book, pens and document folder.
  • □ Appropriate stationery or calculator if required by the course.
  • □ PPE only as instructed by the academy and relevant site.
  • □ Access to approved course notes, practical workbooks and assessments.

Study materials

Visit the Student Resources page for printable introductory course study packs, revision questions and stationery templates. Equipment-specific operating procedures must come from verified manufacturer instructions and qualified training staff.

ACADEMIC APPROACH / 09

Learning with purpose

Training should connect theory, workplace safety and supervised practice.

A sound learning pathway begins with terminology and principles, progresses to hazard awareness and practical preparation, and concludes with approved assessment where applicable.

What to confirm

Ask the academy to confirm the exact course syllabus, practical arrangements, training hours, instructor qualifications, assessment requirements and learning support available for the programme you select.

Suggested enquiry checklist

  • □ Confirm the exact programme and intended outcome.
  • □ Ask for current written requirements and terms.
  • □ Verify practical training and assessment arrangements.
  • □ Confirm the issuer and recognition of any completion document.

CAREER PLANNING / 10

Choosing your training pathway

Different occupations have different entry requirements and competency expectations.

Machine and mining candidates should enquire about equipment-specific training, workplace authorisation and medical or site requirements. Artisan candidates should clarify trade routes and practical experience. Safety candidates should verify the scope of each credential. Business and ICT candidates should enquire about software, assignments and workplace applications.

What to confirm

A course completion document does not automatically establish occupational registration, a licence, employment eligibility or a trade qualification. Verify the credential required for your intended role.
